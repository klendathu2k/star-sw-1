## Notes

- Each column group represents years with identical active-detector configurations,
  based on the first production (`a`-variant) tag for each year (e.g. `y2018a`), or
  the latest variant when no `a` tag exists.
- Infrastructure and passive volumes (`cave`, `pipe`, `magp`, `scon`, `idsm`, `pxst`,
  `supo`, `ftro`, `targ`, `plat`, `pres`, etc.) are excluded as they carry no detector hits.
- **SSD** (`sisd`) served as part of SVT-era tracking (2004–07) and later re-appeared as
  the outermost layer of the HFT system (2014–16).
- **FPD/FMS** (`fpdm`) was absent from the 2014 production geometry and retired after 2018
  when the FCS replaced forward calorimetry.
- **FCS** (Forward Calorimeter System) replaced the FMS starting in 2021, comprising the
  ECal (`wcal`) and HCal (`hcal`).
